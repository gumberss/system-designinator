# Morocco travel basics

Source: curated learning material for the AI Travel Marketplace; revision is
the SHA-256 digest calculated during ingestion.

Morocco offers distinct urban, coastal, mountain, and desert regions. A
traveler interested in architecture and markets might compare time in
Marrakesh and Fes, while a longer route could include coastal or mountain
areas. Distances, terrain, and seasonal conditions make it important to plan
travel time rather than judging a route only by map distance.

Weather, road conditions, local transport, opening times, entry requirements,
and tour availability vary. Check current official government and operator
information before making plans. This guide does not provide live safety,
weather, transport, price, or availability information.

In markets, ask permission before photographing people or private spaces.
Dress and visit expectations can differ by location; follow local guidance
and respect requests at religious or community sites.
