# Japan travel basics

Source: curated learning material for the AI Travel Marketplace; revision is
the SHA-256 digest calculated during ingestion.

Japan combines large urban centers with regional destinations. Tokyo is a
major transport hub; Kyoto is known for historic districts, temples, and
traditional gardens. Travelers can compare destinations by season, travel
time, interests, and accessibility needs rather than assuming one itinerary
fits everyone.

Rail and local transit routes, operating times, fares, entry rules, and
accessibility arrangements change. Check the current official transport,
venue, and government sources before travel. This guide contains no live
schedule, price, visa, weather, or availability information.

For a considerate visit, follow posted rules at shrines, temples, gardens, and
residential areas. Ask before photographing people, keep voices low where
requested, and check local guidance for waste sorting and footwear customs.
These are general reminders, not a substitute for venue-specific instructions.
