# Italy travel basics

Source: curated learning material for the AI Travel Marketplace; revision is
the SHA-256 digest calculated during ingestion.

Italy offers varied city and regional experiences. Rome is often chosen for
ancient sites and museums; Florence for Renaissance art and architecture;
Venice for its lagoon setting; and smaller towns for regional food and local
heritage. Travel pace, mobility, heat sensitivity, and time for rest can shape
a practical itinerary.

Museum opening hours, timed-entry rules, transit service, local access
restrictions, and admission prices may change. Verify current details with
official venue and local government sources. This guide does not provide
current prices, schedules, ticket availability, or legal travel advice.

Historic centers may have restricted vehicle zones and uneven surfaces.
Confirm accessibility directly with venues and transport operators, and
leave time between activities rather than assuming transfers are immediate.
